# VIVA QUESTIONS for VentureLens Project

1. What is the primary purpose of the VentureLens application?
   - The primary purpose of VentureLens is to assist venture capitalists in screening startup investment opportunities by providing a structured, evidence-grounded analysis against a user-defined investment thesis.

2. How does Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) work in this application?
   - RAG combines traditional retrieval methods with generative models to fetch relevant evidence from indexed documents and generate responses based on that evidence, ensuring that the information is grounded in actual data.

3. What is Agentic AI, and how is it implemented in VentureLens?
   - Agentic AI refers to the system's ability to assist human decision-making by orchestrating specialized analytical tasks. In VentureLens, it is implemented through various analytical agents that perform specific analyses on startup data.

4. Can you explain the importance of distinguishing between negative evidence and missing evidence?
   - Distinguishing between negative evidence and missing evidence is crucial because negative evidence indicates a weakness relative to the investment thesis, while missing evidence simply means that information is unavailable and should not be interpreted as a weakness.

5. What types of documents can be uploaded to the VentureLens application?
   - Users can upload various types of documents, including pitch decks, business plans, financial models, founder profiles, market research, product information, and other supporting documents.

6. How does the application handle document processing and chunking?
   - The application extracts text from uploaded documents, cleans unnecessary whitespace, splits the text into meaningful chunks, attaches metadata, generates embeddings, and stores them in ChromaDB for retrieval.

7. What is the role of the Investment Thesis Builder in VentureLens?
   - The Investment Thesis Builder allows users to define their investment criteria, including preferred sectors, geographies, stages, and other factors, which the application uses to evaluate startups against the defined thesis.

8. How does the application generate due-diligence questions for founders?
   - Based on identified information gaps and contradictions, the application generates specific, evidence-oriented questions that are relevant to the startup's data and the user's investment thesis.

9. What are the key components of the structured Startup Screening Report generated by VentureLens?
   - The report includes sections such as executive summary, company overview, investment thesis alignment, business model analysis, market opportunity, traction, financial analysis, founder/team analysis, competitive landscape, key strengths, potential risks, and due-diligence questions.

10. How does the application ensure transparency in its analyses and recommendations?
    - The application provides concise reasoning summaries for its analyses, clearly labeling assumptions, calculations, and evidence used, while avoiding hidden chain-of-thought processes.

11. What measures are in place to handle errors in the application?
    - The application includes error handling for various scenarios, such as missing or invalid API keys, unsupported file types, and insufficient financial data, ensuring that users are informed of issues without fabricating information.

12. How does the application support a human-in-the-loop design?
    - VentureLens is designed as a decision support system, making it clear that final investment decisions remain with the human investor, while the application provides evidence-based screening support.

13. What is the significance of the Investment Committee mode in the application?
    - The Investment Committee mode simulates multiple analytical perspectives, allowing for a comprehensive review of the startup from different angles, which aids in making more informed investment decisions.

14. How does the application perform scenario analysis?
    - If sufficient financial information is available, the application allows users to pose scenario questions and calculates potential outcomes based on available financial inputs, clearly labeling assumptions.

15. What are the limitations of the VentureLens application?
    - Limitations include reliance on the quality and completeness of submitted documents, potential inaccuracies in financial data, and the need for human verification of certain analyses and conclusions.
