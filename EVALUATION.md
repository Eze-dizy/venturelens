# EVALUATION.md

## Evaluation Framework for VentureLens

This document outlines the evaluation framework for assessing the performance and effectiveness of the VentureLens application. The evaluation will focus on several key metrics to ensure that the application meets its objectives and provides valuable insights for venture capital screening.

### Evaluation Metrics

1. **Retrieval Relevance**
   - Measure the accuracy of the evidence retrieved from the document database in relation to the queries made by the agents.

2. **Source Citation Accuracy**
   - Assess the correctness of the citations provided for each claim made in the screening report, ensuring that all claims are traceable to the original documents.

3. **Evidence Grounding**
   - Evaluate how well the application grounds its claims in the evidence retrieved, ensuring that all statements are supported by documented facts.

4. **Contradiction Detection**
   - Test the system's ability to identify contradictions in the information provided across different documents, ensuring that inconsistencies are flagged for review.

5. **Missing-Information Detection**
   - Measure the effectiveness of the application in identifying gaps in the information provided by startups, ensuring that critical data is not overlooked.

6. **Calculation Accuracy**
   - Assess the accuracy of financial calculations performed by the application, ensuring that all metrics are computed correctly based on the provided data.

7. **Thesis Alignment Accuracy**
   - Evaluate how well the startup's information aligns with the user's defined investment thesis, ensuring that the application accurately reflects the user's preferences.

8. **Question Relevance**
   - Measure the relevance and specificity of the due-diligence questions generated by the application, ensuring that they are tailored to the specific gaps and contradictions identified.

9. **Answer Completeness**
   - Assess the completeness of the responses provided by the application, ensuring that all relevant aspects of the startup's information are covered in the analyses.

### Future Evaluation

If actual evaluation is not performed during the initial implementation, a framework for future evaluation should be established. This framework will guide the assessment of the application as it evolves and new features are added. Regular evaluations will help maintain the quality and reliability of the VentureLens application, ensuring it continues to meet the needs of venture capitalists effectively.
